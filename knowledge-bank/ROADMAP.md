# Knowledge Bank Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- API directories and OpenAPI sources
- AI/model/tool directories
- research and dataset discovery
- provider-neutral adapter architecture
- security and secret-handling rules

## Phase 2 — High-value technical inventory
- LLM and multimodal providers
- vision/OCR/product understanding
- image/video/audio generation
- speech and translation
- web search and data extraction
- agents, MCP and automation
- vector databases, RAG and evaluation
- observability and testing

## Phase 3 — Business capability inventory
- ecommerce/catalog/pricing/reviews
- advertising and social APIs
- payments
- delivery/tracking
- maps/geocoding
- analytics
- CRM/support/communications

## Phase 4 — Broad knowledge expansion
- cybersecurity and privacy
- finance and market data
- education
- design/media
- science and engineering
- robotics/IoT
- open data and government datasets
- emerging technologies

## Rule

Do not chase volume for its own sake. The goal is a searchable, continuously expandable knowledge bank that lets us discover the best available option for a problem quickly.

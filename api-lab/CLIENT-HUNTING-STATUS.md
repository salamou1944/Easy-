## Rule for future client hunting
Never label a public listing as an incoming offer. Track separately:
1. Found opportunity
2. Application/proposal submitted
3. Client replied
4. Interview/call
5. Proposal accepted
6. Invoice/payment
7. Delivery
8. Recurring client

The goal of `mony` is to move opportunities through these states quickly and preserve evidence for each state.
